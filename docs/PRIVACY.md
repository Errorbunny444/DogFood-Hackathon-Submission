# Privacy Policy Template

*Last updated: September 2026*

This Privacy Policy outlines how DOGFOOD ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards personal information when operated by an event organizer or hosted service provider.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
- Email address, full name, and display name provided during registration.
- Cryptographically hashed passwords (using Argon2id). We never store raw passwords.
- Optional multi-factor authentication (MFA) credentials (TOTP secret keys).

### B. Hackathon Submissions & Participation
- Team names, member affiliations, and roles.
- Submitted project titles, summaries, descriptions, repository links, and live demonstration URLs.
- Uploaded media assets (project screenshots, logos, design documents).

### C. Judging & Evaluations
- Numerical rubric scores, qualitative feedback comments, and confidence rankings submitted by assigned judges.
- Mathematical calibration records (normalization shrinkage, rank shifts).

### D. System & Audit Logs
- IP addresses, user agent strings, and timestamps associated with critical security events (login, submission, review submission, score publication) stored in append-only audit tables.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

- **Event Administration**: To enable participant registration, team formation, project submissions, and judging assignment.
- **Fair Judging & Integrity**: To detect judge bias, calculate normalized scores, and provide organizers with auditable score provenance.
- **Platform Security**: To detect brute-force attacks, prevent Sybil community voting, and enforce tenant isolation.
- **Communications**: To send account verification tokens, password reset links, team invitations, and competition deadline reminders.

We **do not** sell personal information or track users across external third-party advertising networks.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Visibility

- **Public Showcase**: When an event organizer publishes submissions, project titles, team names, summaries, media, and repository/demo links become visible on the public event gallery.
- **Leaderboards**: Official final rankings and awards are displayed publicly only after explicit review and publication by event organizers.
- **Judge Privacy**: Individual judge scores and reviewer identities remain confidential to prevent external lobbying, retaliation, or conflict of interest. Organizers retain full audit access.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

- Account holders may update their profile or request account deletion through account settings.
- Self-hosted instances retain data according to the server operator's local policies.
